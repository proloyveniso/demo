1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site owners.

Similar technologies — such as web beacons, pixels, local storage, and session storage — may also be used alongside cookies and are referred to collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Enable core website functionality and maintain security.
  • Remember your preferences and personalise your experience.
  • Understand how visitors use our Services (analytics).
  • Measure the effectiveness of our content and features.
  • Deliver relevant advertising to users who visit our platforms.
  • Prevent fraud and improve security across our platforms.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential

Strictly Necessary

Required for the website to function. Cannot be disabled. Includes session management, login state, and security tokens.

Functional

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences such as language, region, and UI choices. Help us provide a personalised experience.

Analytics

Performance / Analytics

Collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site — pages visited, time on page, error events. Used to improve the experience.

Marketing

Marketing / Targeting

Track browsing activity across sites to deliver tailored advertisements. Placed by us or our advertising partners.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Services are set by third parties we work with, including:

  • Google Analytics: Analytics and audience insights. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads / DoubleClick: Advertising and conversion tracking.
  • Facebook / Meta Pixel: Social advertising and audience retargeting.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: B2B audience insights and campaign measurement.
  • Cloudflare: Security, DDoS mitigation, and performance. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

These third parties have their own privacy policies and we have no control over their cookies. We recommend reviewing their policies directly.

5. Cookie Duration

Cookies can be either session cookies or persistent cookies:

  • Session cookies are temporary and deleted when you close your browser. They enable navigation between pages without you having to log in repeatedly.
  • Persistent cookies remain on your device for a defined period (from minutes to years) or until you delete them. They help us remember your preferences on return visits.

6. Your Cookie Choices

Except for strictly necessary cookies, you have the right to choose whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by:

  • Adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • Opting out of interest-based advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Using browser extensions that block tracking technologies.
  • Opting out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of our Services.

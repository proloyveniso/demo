Frequently Asked
Managed SaaS · Managed ITES · Telco Subscription · Consumer

Answers, in plain language.

Short, factual answers about VENISO's Managed SaaS platforms, Managed ITES services, telecom subscription capabilities and consumer products.

What does VENISO do?

VENISO Solutions Private Limited is a Managed SaaS and Managed ITES company founded in 2012. Three segments: Telco VAS (MOITRIBE gaming subscription, M-Health), Managed Services (VENISO SDP, MTRUSS payment gateway SaaS, ADMOFI content management SaaS, MOITRIBE multiplayer engine), and Consumer platforms (MUDGAMES, VPLAY video OTT).

What is VENISO's Managed SaaS offering?

VENISO's Managed SaaS covers four B2B platforms: VENISO SDP (telco-grade Service Delivery Platform), MTRUSS (payment gateway covering DCB, USSD, SMS, cards, wallets, UPI), ADMOFI (content management and ad monetisation) and MOITRIBE Engine (Gaming-as-a-Service multiplayer engine). All delivered as zero-CAPEX managed services with 99.99% uptime SLA.

What is Managed ITES and how does VENISO deliver it?

Managed ITES (IT-enabled Services) means end-to-end management of technology platforms on behalf of clients — not just software, but operations, integrations, support and SLA management too. VENISO delivers VENISO SDP, MTRUSS, ADMOFI, MOITRIBE Engine and VPLAY as fully managed ITES engagements: the operator or partner gets the capability, VENISO runs it.

What is the VENISO SDP?

VENISO SDP is a telco-grade Service Delivery Platform with multi-protocol interconnect (SS7, SIGTRAN, Diameter, REST), unified charging engine (online/offline, real-time balance, 500 RPS peak), VAS orchestration (USSD, IVR, push campaigns), content platform, subscription and package manager, analytics and BI, notification engine, and ISO 27001 security. Delivered as Managed SaaS with zero CAPEX.

What is MTRUSS?

MTRUSS is VENISO's payment gateway Managed SaaS — covering DCB, USSD and SMS for telecom rails, plus cards, wallets, UPI, net banking and PayPal for non-telco rails. One unified subscription engine with grace periods, retries and multi-surface state sync. 15Bn+ transactions processed, 150+ operators connected, 500 RPS peak, 99.99% uptime.

What is ADMOFI?

ADMOFI is VENISO's Content Management Managed SaaS. Two layers: content management (bulk upload, metadata, cloud transcoding, multi-surface delivery across web, WAP, app, PWA, HTML5, STB) and ad monetisation (programmatic mediation across 25+ ad networks, VAST tags, in-game and in-app advertising, yield optimisation). Launched 2013, delivered as a fully managed service.

What is MOITRIBE?

MOITRIBE has two roles. As Telco VAS: a gaming subscription service — eat-all-you-can access to 140+ HTML5 titles plus tournament-based competitive play, billed via DCB. As a Managed Service: the MOITRIBE Engine is a Gaming-as-a-Service multiplayer engine for developers and operators — real-time, turn-based, score tournaments, team tournaments, private rooms and low-bandwidth resilience. Codeless to include on Android and HTML5.

What is VPLAY?

VPLAY is VENISO's video OTT consumer platform. Six monetisation models: SVOD, TVOD, AVOD, PVOD, Catch-Up TV and Coupons. Eleven distribution channels including Google Play, iOS, Amazon Firestick, Jio STB, Airtel Xtreme and PWA. Pre-integrated with 150+ telecoms via DCB across 100+ countries. Live in 30 days.

What is MUDGAMES?

MUDGAMES (also branded OBIGINGAMES) is VENISO's advertisement-powered hyper-casual gaming platform — 140+ titles across 9 genres, playable instantly in any browser with no download required. Monetised through ADMOFI in-game advertising. Powered by the MOITRIBE multiplayer engine.

What is VENISO's M-Health service?

VENISO's M-Health service (Top Doctors Online) is a virtual hospital delivered to telecom subscribers — Educate (Healthopedia in local languages), Consult (10+ specialities, 24×7 on chat, voice, video and live sessions), and Diagnose (home lab collection, digital reports, free doctor follow-up). Managed end-to-end as a Telco VAS Managed ITES.

Which telecom operators does VENISO work with?

VENISO has deployed across 150+ telecom operators in 100+ countries, including Vodafone, Airtel, Idea, Jio, Zain, du, Etisalat, Ooredoo, MPT, Ncell, NT, Orange and WE, among others.

How many DCB transactions has VENISO processed?

Over 15 billion direct carrier billing (DCB) transactions, serving 1 billion+ unique users, processing 40 million telco transactions per day at a peak billing rate of 500 RPS.

Is VENISO ISO 27001 certified?

Yes. VENISO Solutions Private Limited holds ISO 27001 Information Security Management certification, ensuring enterprise-grade data security across all platforms, deployments and client data environments.

How can I partner with VENISO or integrate its platforms?

Contact VENISO at business@veniso.com. Platforms are designed for rapid deployment — typically live in weeks, not quarters — with whitelabel branding, operator-grade SLAs and zero CAPEX for telecom and media partners.

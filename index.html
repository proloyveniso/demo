06 · Frequently Asked
For partners, buyers and search engines

Answers, in plain language.

Short, factual answers about VENISO's B2B Managed SaaS platform and the three consumer services — MOITRIBE, M-Health and MUDGAMES.

What does VENISO do?

VENISO is a technology-driven products company founded in 2012 that builds proprietary platforms powering telecom operators and consumer services — a Managed SaaS for CMS, package management and billing, plus three consumer products: MOITRIBE (gaming subscriptions), M-Health / Top Doctors Online (virtual hospital) and MUDGAMES (ad-powered hyper-casual gaming).

What is VENISO's B2B Managed SaaS?

It gives telecom operators and digital partners three integrated capabilities: (1) CMS and app configuration for hosting and distributing content, (2) a package manager for offers, promotions and subscription plans, and (3) a unified billing and subscription engine covering direct carrier billing and non-telco rails like cards, wallets and UPI. Delivered as a managed service, cloud-hosted, with zero CAPEX.

What is MOITRIBE?

MOITRIBE is VENISO's telecom VAS gaming subscription service offering two commercial models — an eat-all-you-can subscription to 100+ HTML5 games, and a tournament-based competitive layer with leaderboards, chat and a rewards economy. It runs on a codeless multiplayer engine that any operator, media brand or device partner can adopt.

What is MUDGAMES?

MUDGAMES is VENISO's advertisement-powered hyper-casual gaming platform. It hosts 127 titles across 9 genres, playable instantly in any browser with no download, and monetises through in-game advertising powered by VENISO's proprietary ADMOFI mediation platform.

What is VENISO's M-Health service?

VENISO's M-Health service (Top Doctors Online) is a virtual hospital delivered to telecom subscribers. Three pillars — Educate (Healthopedia medical content in local languages), Consult (10+ specialities, 24×7 on chat, voice, video and live sessions), and Diagnose (home lab collection with digital reports and a free doctor follow-up). Managed end-to-end in markets where the telco runs the service with permission.

Which telecom operators does VENISO work with?

VENISO has deployed across 150+ telecom operators in 50+ countries, including Vodafone, Airtel, Idea, Jio, Zain, du, Etisalat, Ooredoo, MPT, Ncell, NT, Orange and WE, among others.

How many DCB transactions has VENISO processed?

Over 15 billion direct carrier billing (DCB) transactions, serving 1 billion+ unique users, processing 40 million telco transactions per day at a peak billing rate of 500 RPS.

How does VENISO handle billing and subscriptions?

Through an in-house proprietary unified billing engine routing every transaction across telco rails (DCB, USSD, SMS, zero-rated portals) and non-telco rails (cards, wallets, UPI, bank transfer, PayPal). It supports multi-route paywalls, dynamic plans, retries, grace periods and subscription state synced across app, web, TV and partner stores.
